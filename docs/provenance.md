# Public-source provenance and interpretation limits

Screen performed September 18, 2026. This project uses publicly available scientific literature to motivate generic mechanisms. It contains no nonpublic measurements, internal protocols, named experimental target, or proprietary parameter estimates.

## Scope of the screen

The retrieved full-text records were inspected for author affiliations, funding, acknowledgments, declarations of interest, and excluded-affiliation text. No explicitly excluded affiliation was identified in the retrieved text of the core full-text references below. This is a publication-record screen, not a complete institutional relationship investigation, guarantee of current independence, intellectual-property clearance, or legal advice. Absent or incomplete disclosures are not treated as proof of no connection.

Neither the numerical defaults nor the synthetic examples were extracted from unpublished observations. All numerical model values are labeled illustrative, and source claims are kept separate from the mathematical construction.

## Technical assay-definition check

The publicly available [membrane-integrity DNA-dye technical protocol](https://www.promega.com/resources/protocols/technical-manuals/101/celltox-green-cytotoxicity-assay-protocol/) describes exclusion from viable cells and increased signal after binding DNA in membrane-compromised cells. The [full TM375 manual, revised July 2023](https://www.promega.de/-/media/files/resources/protocols/technical-manuals/101/celltox-green-cytotoxicity-assay-protocol.pdf), was also checked for measurement interference. It identifies compound autofluorescence, quenching, competing DNA-intercalating compounds, and DNA fragments in extracellular matrix as possible interferences. Manufacturer documentation is not peer-reviewed evidence for a hook mechanism, a source of fitted parameters, or evidence for the optional reporter-loss scenario.

The numerical consequences were checked separately from literature support. `tools/review_checks.py` reproduces finite-receptor mass balances, the secondary-excess example, review-only dynamic trajectories, and the payload-scale nonidentifiability example; outputs are in `results/review-checks.json`. These checks verify consequences of the stated mathematics, not the biology of an experimental assay.

## Sources considered but not used as independent foundations

The [2004 secondary-conjugate paper](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/bc049969t) is historically relevant, but the retrieved publisher content did not expose a sufficiently complete affiliation/funding/conflict record. It is not used as an independently cleared core reference here. A separate [ternary-equilibrium mathematical paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7650257/) was read for context, but the simplified equilibrium expression in this repository is explicitly derived from the model's own independent-binding assumptions.

No source is claimed to prove the model's numerical examples. No source-screening statement guarantees freedom from legal risk.
